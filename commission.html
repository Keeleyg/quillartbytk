    What to include in your brief

    • Description or subject — what should the piece depict?
    • Preferred size (e.g. small ~A4, medium ~A3, large ~A2, or specific dimensions)
    • Colour preferences — any palette in mind? Colours to avoid?
    • Format / medium (framed under glass, canvas, clock face, card, other?)
    • Personalisation — names, dates, or words to include?
    • Reference photos — inspiration pieces, photos of the subject, your space
    • Budget range (optional, but helps me suggest options that fit)
    • Deadline or occasion (optional)
