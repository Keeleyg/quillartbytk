A charming minimalist framed quilling of two cats — one white, one black
— sitting side by side with their tails curving upward and meeting to
form a heart shape above them. Each cat's body is constructed from
tightly nested paper spirals, with delicate whisker detailing on each
face. A sentimental piece for cat lovers, couples, anniversaries, or
'just because' gifts. Mounted in a clean white shadow-box frame.
