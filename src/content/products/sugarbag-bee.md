A scientifically-styled framed quilled rendering of the Australian
native stingless Sugarbag Bee (Tetragonula carbonaria), featuring a
glossy black body with silver-thread filigree thorax detail, lacy
translucent layered wings with intricate vein patterns, and the species
name printed beneath the specimen on a clean white card. Mounted in a
deep white shadow-box frame. Part of the native bees series alongside
P014 (Blue Banded) and P121 (Domino Cuckoo); also available as a
triptych (P122).
