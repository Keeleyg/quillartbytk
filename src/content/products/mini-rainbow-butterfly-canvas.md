A vibrant quilled butterfly with rainbow-coloured wings (red, orange,
yellow, green, blue, purple) and a dimensional white body, mounted on a
miniature stretched canvas. Each wing features dense rolled-paper
spirals of varying sizes. A cheerful gift-scale piece for desks,
shelves, kitchens, or children's spaces. Comes with a small wooden
easel. Part of the mini-canvas series alongside the Mandala (P048),
Rainbow & Hearts (P049), Rainbow Garden (P055), Sunflower (P059), and
Ladybug Vine (P067) designs.
