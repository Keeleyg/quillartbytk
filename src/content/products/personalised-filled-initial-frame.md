A made-to-order personalised initial piece featuring a single tall block
letter cut as an outline and filled with rolled-paper quilling in the
customer's chosen palette — the example shown is a serif "L" filled with
swirling blue, navy and pale lilac spirals with small floral accents.
Mounted in a white square shadow-box frame. Single-initial sibling to
the multi-letter Lucian style (P077); structurally distinct from P038
which sets a white letter against a quilled background.
