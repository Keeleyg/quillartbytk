A three-aperture white shadow-box frame spelling out the message 'Give
Yourself Time', with each word paired with a stage of butterfly
metamorphosis: 'Give' with a striped monarch caterpillar on grass,
'Yourself' with a hanging green-and-gold chrysalis on a leafy branch,
and 'Time' with the fully-emerged orange-and-black monarch butterfly. A
gentle, meditative inspirational piece for offices, healing spaces,
study walls, or anywhere a quiet reminder of patience and growth would
be welcome.
