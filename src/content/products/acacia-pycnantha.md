A botanical-illustration-style framed quilling of the Australian
national floral emblem Acacia pycnantha (golden wattle) — a sinuous stem
with paired grey-green elongated leaves and clusters of fringed
golden-yellow pompom flowers. Mounted in a white shadow-box frame with a
printed scientific-name label. Companion piece to P052 (Corymbia
ficifolia) and P075 (Callistemon citrinus) in the native-botanicals
series.
