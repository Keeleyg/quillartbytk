A dramatic art-style framed quilling of a yellow-tailed black cockatoo,
head-on and brooding — the head a dense field of black filigree spirals,
piercing yellow eyes, a heavy curved black beak, and a long fanned tail
of mustard-yellow and black plumage. Mounted against a black background
in a deep black shadow-box frame. A more graphic, stylised counterpart
to the naturalistic P011 and P012.
