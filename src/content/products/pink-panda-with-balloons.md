A cheerful framed quilling of a baby panda with the classic
black-and-white pattern, soft pink ear interiors and cheek-blush
spirals, a pink bow atop the head, a pink pompom tail accent, and a
cluster of three pink balloons held by hand-drawn strings. Mounted in a
clean white shadow-box frame. The third piece in the Nursery Animals
with Balloons series — pairs with the Pink Elephant (P042) and Brown
Monkey (P043) to form a coordinated trio. A blue/boy palette variant is
available on request — please specify when enquiring.
