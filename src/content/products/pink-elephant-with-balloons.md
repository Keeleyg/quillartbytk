A cheerful framed quilling of a baby elephant with grey body, soft pink
ear interiors, a pink bow atop her head, and a cluster of five pink
balloons held by hand-drawn strings tied to her tail. The trunk lifts
joyfully upward. Mounted in a clean white shadow-box frame. A favourite
for baby-girl nurseries; a blue/boy palette variant is available on
request — please specify when enquiring.
