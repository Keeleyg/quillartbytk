A single large dimensional quilled feather mounted in a clean white
shadow-box frame. The feather is built from densely packed gradient
ribbons of paper that fan out from a central white spine. Available in
palette variants: cool (navy/blue/teal/green/cream) and warm (deep
burgundy/red/orange/peach/cream). A bold, painterly statement piece.
