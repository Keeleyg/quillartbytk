A single dense quilled sunflower with layered yellow petals and a
dimensional brown centre, surrounded by curling green tendrils, mounted
on a miniature stretched canvas displayed on its own little wooden
easel. A cheerful, gift-scale piece for desks, kitchens, or small wall
arrangements. Comes with the wooden easel. Part of the mini-canvas
series alongside the Mandala (P048), Rainbow & Hearts (P049), Butterfly
(P054), Rainbow Garden (P055), and Ladybug Vine (P067) designs.
