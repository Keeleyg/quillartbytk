A large-scale abstract framed quilling in a soft sea-glass palette —
layered ribbons of sage, eucalyptus, cream and dusty teal curl and
overlap to create a flowing, calligraphic composition with substantial
dimensional depth. Mounted in a clean light oak frame. A statement piece
for a hallway, lounge, or above a console table — distinctly different
in mood from the figurative and themed work in the rest of the
collection.
