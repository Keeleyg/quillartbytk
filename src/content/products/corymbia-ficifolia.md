A botanical-illustration-style framed quilling of three blossoms of the
Australian native Corymbia ficifolia (red flowering gum) — vivid pink
fringed flowers with golden-yellow stamens, hanging from a brown stem
with three sage-green eucalyptus leaves. Includes a scientific name
plaque. Part of the same natural-history-specimen aesthetic family as
the Blue Banded Bee (P014) and the Framed Beetle (P036) — suited to
studies, libraries, or anywhere an Australian native botanical accent is
welcome. NOTE: the scientific name label currently reads 'Corumbia'
(typo) — should be 'Corymbia'. Worth correcting before this listing goes
live.
