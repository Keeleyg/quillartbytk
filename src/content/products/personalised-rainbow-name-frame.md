A made-to-order personalised name piece featuring crisp white block
letters set against a vibrant rainbow background — each letter is filled
with a riot of tightly-rolled paper spirals in every colour of the
rainbow, with floral and heart accents tucked between the strokes. The
example shown spells "Lucian" with a small heart dot above the "i". A
long landscape white shadow-box frame. Custom orders made to the
customer's chosen name; full-name version companion to P038
(single-initial floral) and P108 (single-initial filled).
