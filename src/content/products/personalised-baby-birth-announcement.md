A made-to-order personalised birth announcement frame featuring a
dimensional quilled teddy bear (palette to suit — this sample shown in
pink, also available in blue, neutral, or your choice of colours), four
floating balloons bearing the baby's birth statistics (length, weight,
date, time of birth), and the baby's name spelled out in coordinating
quilled letters on a small card below. A meaningful, keepsake gift for a
baby shower, christening, naming day, or first birthday — designed to be
displayed in the nursery for years. Please provide the baby's full name,
date of birth, time of birth, weight, length, and any palette
preferences when ordering. This piece is a commission example and is
offered as a custom order only; please allow 3–4 weeks for completion.
