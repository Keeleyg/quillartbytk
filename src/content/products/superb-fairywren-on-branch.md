A male Superb Fairywren beautifully rendered in quilled paper, perched
on a textured brown branch with green foliage. The bird's distinctive
electric blue cap and cheek patches contrast with deep navy on the back
and breast, while warm tan and cream tones detail the wing and
underside. The long blue tail rises elegantly above the body. Presented
in a clean white shadow-box frame.
