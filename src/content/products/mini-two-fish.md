A small framed quilling of two cheerful round fish swimming downward —
each with a blue ring body packed with concentric blue and teal coil
scales, a black eye dot, and pale-mint quilled tail fins — accompanied
by a trail of small blue bubble dots and a pair of dimensional green
seaweed fronds at the base. Mounted in a clean white standard shadow-box
frame. Part of the mini sea-creatures sub-series alongside P125
(Jellyfish) and P126 (Seahorse).
