A wide triptych shadow-box frame in light oak presenting three native
Australian bee quillings side by side, each with its printed
scientific-name label: Sugarbag Bee (Tetragonula carbonaria), Blue
Banded Bee (Amegilla cingulata), and Domino Cuckoo Bee (Thyreus
lugubris). The individual pieces (P120, P014, P121) are also available
separately.
