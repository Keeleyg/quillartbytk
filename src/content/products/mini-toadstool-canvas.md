A pair of classic red-and-white-spotted toadstools (one large, one
small) with cream stems, growing out of a tuft of dimensional green
grass blades. Each cap is densely quilled with red on-edge paper banding
and white spot accents. Mounted on a miniature stretched canvas. Part of
the mini-canvas series.
