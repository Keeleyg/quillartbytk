A scientifically-styled framed quilled rendering of the European Honey
Bee (Apis mellifera), featuring a yellow-and-black banded abdomen with
metallic gold thread detail, a glossy black thorax with gold filigree,
layered cream-paper wings with intricate spiral vein patterns, and the
species name printed beneath the specimen on a clean white card.
Available in white-frame and black-frame options. Companion piece to the
native bees series (P014, P120, P121, P122) covering the introduced
honey bee.
