large four-leaf clover and two smaller three-leaf clovers) growing from

LUCK' on a small card below. A motivational St Patrick's Day or
Irish-themed gift suited to desks, shelves, or small wall arrangements;
reads year-round as a 'good fortune' piece rather than strictly
