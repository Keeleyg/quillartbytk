A pair of vivid red quilled ladybugs perched on a curving green
leaf-vine, mounted on a miniature stretched canvas displayed on its own
little wooden easel. Each ladybug features dimensional spiral wing
detail, black spots, and tiny antennae; the vine has eight rolled-paper
leaves curling along its length. A cheerful, gift-scale piece for desks,
kitchens, garden-themed rooms, or children's spaces. Comes with the
wooden easel. Part of the mini-canvas series alongside the Mandala
(P048), Rainbow & Hearts (P049), Butterfly (P054), Rainbow Garden
(P055), and Sunflower (P059) designs.
