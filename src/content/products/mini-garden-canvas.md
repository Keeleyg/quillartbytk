A whimsical quilled garden scene with eight tightly-rolled flower
blossoms in rainbow colours (green spiral, purple, pink heart-flower,
mustard, red spiral, red poppies, pink, green spiral) on slender green
stems, joined by a small yellow butterfly. Surrounded by curving green
grass blades and mounted on a miniature stretched canvas. A more
populated companion variant to P055 (Mini Rainbow Garden Canvas) in the
mini-canvas series.
