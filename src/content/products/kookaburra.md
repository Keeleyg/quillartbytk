brown branch — soft cream-and-lavender chest, brown-and-grey wing with
blue accent feathers, the distinctive long dark beak, and an
orange-and-cream-banded tail. Mounted in a light oak shadow-box frame
with a generous white mat. Part of the Australian birds series alongside
the Sulphur-crested Cockatoo (P080) and Galah (P083).
