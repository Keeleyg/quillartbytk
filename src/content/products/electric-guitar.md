A striking framed quilling of an electric guitar in fiery reds, oranges,
and yellows with deep black accents and intricate spiral filigree
throughout the body. Hand-drawn musical notes float on a flowing staff
from the headstock outward. Mounted in a clean white shadow-box frame. A
bold, gift-led piece for a musician, a music room, or a teenager's
bedroom. Also available in a monochrome (black, white and grey) palette
and in a black frame — see additional photos for palette and frame
variants.
