A classical yin-yang symbol rendered in dimensional quilling — one half
built from black filigree spirals with a small lilac dot at its centre,
the other from pale lilac and cream coils with a small black dot —
meeting in flowing wave-like halves. Mounted in a deep black square
shadow-box frame with a generous white mat. A calming, meditation-themed
piece.
