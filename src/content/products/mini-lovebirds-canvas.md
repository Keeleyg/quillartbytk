A pair of quilled lovebirds — one buttery yellow on the left, one hot
pink on the right — leaning toward each other with eyes closed and tiny
red beaks almost touching, with a small two-tone heart floating between
their heads. Each bird is built from elegant teardrop loops and rolled
spirals. Mounted on a miniature stretched canvas. Part of the
mini-canvas series.
