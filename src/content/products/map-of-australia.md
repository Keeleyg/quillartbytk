A large framed quilling of the map of Australia, the continent and
Tasmania filled with intricate filigree spirals in muted heritage tones
— soft blues, dusty mauves, sandy creams, and pale greys with white
accent coils — and a small quilled heart placed centrally over the
inland. Mounted in a light oak shadow-box frame with a generous white
mat. A statement piece for Australian-themed décor.
