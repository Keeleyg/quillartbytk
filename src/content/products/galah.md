A dimensional framed quilling of an Australian galah perched on a brown
branch with eucalyptus leaves — soft pink crest with rolled spiral
detail, deep pink chest with horizontal feather banding, and grey wings
and tail. Mounted in a light oak shadow-box frame. Part of the
Australian birds series alongside the Sulphur-crested Cockatoo (P080)
and Kookaburra (P082).
