A cheerful framed quilling of a baby kangaroo with a soft brown-grey
body, tall pink-lined ears, pink cheek-blush spirals, a black nose and
whiskers, a blue bow tie, and a cluster of four blue-and-white balloons
held by hand-drawn strings. Mounted in a clean white shadow-box frame.
The first proven blue/boy-palette entry in the Nursery Animals with
Balloons series. A pink/girl palette variant or a different animal in
this blue palette is available on request — please specify when
enquiring.
