A graceful quilled angelfish with intricate scrollwork body — layered
blues, teals and silver-greys with dimensional spirals across the fins
and body — accompanied by a small school of teal bubble dots to the
right. Mounted in a rope-edged white shadow-box frame. Part of the
nautical series alongside P030 (Sailboat), P031 (Lighthouse), P032
(Whale), P099 (Dolphin), P100 (Seahorse) and P130 (Stingray).
