A single fern frond rendered in quilled paper with leaflets graduating
through the full rainbow spectrum — deep red at the base, climbing
through orange, yellow, green, blue, purple and pink to white at the
tip. A delicate, contemplative piece with a botanical-specimen feel.
Mounted in a clean white shadow-box frame. Pairs naturally with the
Rainbow Tree of Life (P029) for buyers wanting a coordinated set in the
rainbow palette family.
