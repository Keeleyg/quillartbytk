A striking framed quilling of a dog or cat paw print rendered entirely
in black: one large rounded triangular pad and four oval toe pads, each
filled with intricate spiral filigree and small flower-motif detailing.
Mounted in a clean white shadow-box frame. A meaningful gift for pet
owners, dog/cat lovers, or as a memorial piece for a beloved pet.
Available in white frame (shown here) or black frame (see additional
photo) — please specify when ordering. A rainbow-coloured paw-print
version in a heart frame is also available (see P066).
