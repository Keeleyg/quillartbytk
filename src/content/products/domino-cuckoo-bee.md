A scientifically-styled framed quilled rendering of the Australian
native Domino Cuckoo Bee (Thyreus lugubris), featuring a striking black
body covered in distinct white spot accents, fine quilled-filigree
segmentation, slender outlined wings, and the species name printed
beneath the specimen on a clean white card. Mounted in a deep white
shadow-box frame. Part of the native bees series alongside P014 (Blue
Banded) and P120 (Sugarbag); also available as a triptych (P122).
