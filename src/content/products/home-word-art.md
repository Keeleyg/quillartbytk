A large landscape-oriented framed quilling spelling 'HOME' in ornate
dimensional capitals. Each letter is constructed from layered quilled
spirals in a cream, ivory, charcoal and dark brown palette, with
delicate floral and curling-tendril accents woven through and around the
letters. Mounted on a clean white background with a deep chocolate-brown
wooden outer frame and cream inner mat. A welcoming statement piece for
hallways, entry walls, or above a sideboard; works as a housewarming,
engagement, or new-home gift.
