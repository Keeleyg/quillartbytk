About the Artist

Welcome! I'm TK, and I create handmade quilled paper artworks from my home studio in Australia. Quilling is the art of rolling, shaping, and arranging thin strips of paper into intricate designs — a craft with centuries of history that I've fallen in love with.

Each piece in my collection is made entirely by hand, with no two works ever exactly alike. I draw inspiration from nature, geometry, and the Australian landscape, using vibrant colours and delicate forms to bring paper to life in three dimensions.

What started as a creative hobby has grown into a passion for sharing these unique artworks with others. Every piece takes many hours of careful crafting — from selecting the paper colours to shaping each individual coil and assembling the final composition.
